Security Patrol

Proof of every security round, with secure tags that can’t be faked

Guards tap their phone to a secure checkpoint tag, answer All clear or Report an issue, and the building has a record that stands up. No readers, no scanners, nothing for the guard to install.

✓Signed tags that can’t be copied or faked

The problem

Paper logs and QR codes can be filled in or scanned from anywhere. A code or a sticker can be photographed and ‘checked’ from the break room. A secure checkpoint tag is cryptographically signed, so each check proves the guard was standing there.

How it works
1

Their phone opens the check. There is nothing to install.

2

All clear or an issue

A note and photos if something’s wrong.

3

An issue is emailed to the desk and managers at once.

4

Managers see every round

The patrol log, issues highlighted, and a report to download.
