Visitor parking

Visitor parking that starts with the resident

Residents invite their own guests from their phone. Guests get a digital pass. The desk sees who is parked and can check any car in seconds — no sign-up sheet, no visitor registration, no approvals queue.

✓Residents sign in with their email — no password, no app
✓Guests need no account
✓Works on its own or alongside Rezanto package management
✓Nothing to install
How it works
1

Resident signs in

With an email link, or by scanning the QR code in the lobby.

2

Invites a guest

Picks how long, then shares a link and a 6-digit code by text.

3

Guest parks

Taps the link, or enters the code at the parkade QR, adds their plate and gets a digital pass.

4

Desk sees it all

Parked now, coming up, just expired. Look up a car by plate, car, unit or pass reference.

Core capabilities
1

Digital guest pass

The same link or code brings the pass back if the page is closed.

2

Look up a car

Find a car by plate, car, unit or pass reference and see right away whether it is allowed.

3

Your building’s rules

Overnight to a set time, nights per month, one guest at a time — or no limits. The resident sees the answer as they invite.

4

A record for every pass

The unit is emailed each time a guest parks.

5

QR codes to post

Lobby for residents, parkade for guests with a code. Print-ready.

6

Built for real households

Residents with several units switch between them. End a stay early or resend a pass in one tap.

7

Desk tools

Issue a pass for a walk-up with a resident’s code, extend a stay, and add notes on a plate.
