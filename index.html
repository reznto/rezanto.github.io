Visitor Parking

Residents invite their guests. The desk just checks.

Every visitor pass starts with a resident, so there is no sign-up sheet, no visitor registration and no approvals queue. Guests get a pass on their phone; the desk sees who is parked and can check any car in seconds. Use it on its own or together with Package Management.

Walk through the parking demo north_east

  1. 1

    Resident signs in

    With their email. No password, no app — a QR code in the lobby takes them there.

  2. 2

    Invites a guest

    Picks how long, then shares a link and a 6-digit code by text or any messaging app.

  3. 3

    Guest parks

    Taps the link, enters their plate and gets a digital pass. No account to create.

  4. 4

    Desk sees it all

    Who is parked now, who is coming, and a one-box lookup for any car.

badge

mail

Security Patrol

Proof the round happened. Not just a checkbox.

A QR code or a sticker can be photographed and ‘checked’ from the break room. Rezanto’s secure checkpoint tags are cryptographically signed, so each check proves the guard was there.

    All clear or an issue

    A note and photos if something’s wrong.

  3. 3

    The building hears

    An issue is emailed to the desk and managers at once.

  4. 4

    Managers see every round

    The patrol log, issues highlighted, and a report to download.

Behind Rezanto

Eddie Tang brings 15+ years of B2B technology experience across startups and enterprise software. He built Rezanto to solve the operational headaches property teams face every day — with a platform designed to save time, reduce errors, and improve the resident experience.

Requests go straight to the person who writes the code. Per-unit parcel limits with overage billing, and AI label scanning from a phone, both shipped because a building asked for them — in weeks, not roadmap quarters. That is not something a larger vendor can do for one property.
